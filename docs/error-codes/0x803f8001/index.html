Ownership and license

Xbox license error 0x803F8001

The Xbox app or Microsoft Store may be unable to confirm that the signed-in account is entitled to use the game or app.

Scan license dependencies
Local repair has limits. A healthy PC cannot restore an expired subscription, a removed catalog title, or ownership tied to another account.

What can be checked locally

  • Whether Xbox app and Microsoft Store appear to use a consistent account context.
  • License Manager, ClipSVC, Xbox authentication services, Gaming Services, and recent failures.
  • System time, time zone, region, TLS, account-token, and Store package health.
  • Offline-permission context and signs that the problem is remote or entitlement-related.

How the report separates causes

The tool distinguishes locally repairable service or package findings from user actions and external entitlement conditions. It does not label an account problem fixed after only restarting a service.

Check the PC side of 0x803F8001

Run a focused scan before resetting the Store or reinstalling the game.

When the result may be manual

The report may direct you to verify the purchasing account, subscription status, catalog availability, family controls, or Xbox service status when local evidence is healthy.
