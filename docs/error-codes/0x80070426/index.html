Required service not active

Xbox app error 0x80070426

A required Xbox, Store, account, license, time, update, or Gaming Services dependency may be unavailable when Xbox app starts or signs in.

Scan service dependencies
Stopped does not always mean broken. Trigger-start and manual services may stop normally while idle; the scanner also checks configuration, dependencies, events, and the attempted operation.

What the tool verifies

  • Xbox Live service groups, Gaming Services, Store, license, account, time, BITS, and Windows Update dependencies.
  • Service existence, startup type, trigger behavior, executable path, policy, and recent failures.
  • Package health and missing-component evidence that can explain a service failure.
  • Windows 10/11-aware expectations and post-repair validation.

How repairs are reported

A supported service correction records previous state where possible. The outcome can be success, partial, pending restart, or failed; running a command alone is not counted as proof.
