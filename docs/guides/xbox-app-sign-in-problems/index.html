Account and identity

Xbox app sign-in problems

Diagnose sign-in loops, a login window that disappears, account mismatch, token failure, or license checks that do not complete.

Protect your account. Never enter a Microsoft password into this repair tool or a feedback description.

Symptoms

  • The sign-in dialog closes before authentication completes.
  • Xbox app repeatedly asks for the same account.
  • Microsoft Store and Xbox app show different accounts.
  • Owned or Game Pass content appears unavailable.

Likely causes

Possible layers include Xbox Identity Provider, Microsoft Account Sign-in Assistant, Web Account Manager/token broker, user-session inconsistency, Store licensing, wrong time or region, certificate/TLS failure, family policy, subscription state, or a remote authentication incident.

Safe manual checks

  1. Check Microsoft/Xbox service status.
  2. Verify date, time zone, and region.
  3. Confirm Xbox app and Microsoft Store use the intended account.
  4. Sign out and back in through the official Microsoft interfaces.
  5. Check subscription and family settings directly in the Microsoft account.

What the repair tool checks

The scan inspects Xbox Identity Provider presence, account-token services, user-session consistency, Store license refresh chain, Xbox protocol registration, time and time-zone configuration, region/language context, TLS/certificate evidence, Family Safety local policy signals, and related events.

What can be repaired automatically

  • Repair Windows time synchronization and automatic time-zone service configuration.
  • Start suitable identity, license, Store, and Xbox dependencies.
  • Refresh supported package registrations or Store cache.
  • Prompt the user to sign out and sign in again.

What cannot be repaired automatically

The tool cannot unlock an account, reset a password, change subscription or payment status, remove a family restriction, bypass multi-factor authentication, or repair remote Microsoft identity systems.

Risks and undo support

Time and service settings may be reversible when captured. App reset can sign the user out and clear settings. Account actions remain manual and are not stored as credentials.

Check local identity infrastructure

Use the scan to separate Windows configuration from an account or remote-service issue.
